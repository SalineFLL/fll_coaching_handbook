# Reserving the Media Center for Extra Practices

> **Applies to:** FLL-Challenge & FLL-Explore

To reserve the middle school media center for extra practices (usually on Saturdays), please determine the desired dates and times as early as possible.
Fill in your name and the time you want the media center reserved on the date desired [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1mr4-dkHNwn1WmZ9ezK4v9Yc8L6dZmINBfun5MkqGZOo/edit?usp=sharing). Laurie Dawson (<dawsonl@salineschools.org>), our club's point of contact with the school, will
be notified that you made changes, and she will highlight your reservations when she confirms that you may have the space on your desired days/times. The
highlighting of your requested days/times will be your only indication that your reservation was accepted.
