# Links to FIRST Resources

> **Applies to:** FLL-Challenge & FLL-Explore

## Team management.

Jeremy will do much of this for you, but he will occasionally let you know when you need to take action via your FIRST dashboard to ensure your team can participate in competition.

* [FIRST Dashboard](https://www.firstinspires.org/login)

The FIRST in Michigan website is a wealth of information.
At this site you can find **rulebooks**, **judging rubrics**,
**event readiness information**,
and **team event assignments** for regional and state competitions.

* [FIRST in Michigan](https://firstinmichigan.us/FLL-Challenge/resources/)

## Season materials

* [FLL season materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/fll/season-materials)

This page has mission model build instructions for the season, along with a lot of other useful resources. In particular, both FLL-Challenge and FLL-Explore coaches should check out the **Team Meeting Guide** found in the "Founders Edition Grades 2-4 (Explore)" and "Founders Edition Grades 4-8 (Challenge)" sections -- it's a great resource for planning your season.

Don't be confused by the grade ranges in those section names: they don't apply to our Michigan teams. Go by whether your team is FLL-Explore or FLL-Challenge, not by the grades listed on the firstinspires.org site.
